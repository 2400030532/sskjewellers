Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common showroom and jewellery questions.

Where is Sri Sai Krishna Jewellers located?

Located at Sai Baba Gudi Center, Etikoppaka, AP - 531082 (near Anakapalli & Visakhapatnam). Open daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Call/WhatsApp: +91 9573199344.

Is every gold ornament 916 BIS Hallmarked?

Yes, 100%. Every piece carries certified 22 Karat (916) BIS Hallmark with unique 6-digit HUID laser engraving, verifiable on the BIS Care app.

Can I order custom handmade jewellery?

Yes! Share your design, Pinterest photo, or sketch on WhatsApp (+91 9573199344). Our Etikoppaka master karigars craft it to your exact weight and budget.

Do you offer WhatsApp video shopping?

Yes! Schedule a free 1-on-1 video call to inspect any ornament in high clarity, verify hallmark stamps, and check weight on digital scales live before buying.

Do you sell pure 925 silver pooja items?

Yes! We have a complete collection of pure 925 silver deepams, kalashams, plates, and baby gifts (silver pattilu & chains).
